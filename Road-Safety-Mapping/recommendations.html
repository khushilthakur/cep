Road Safety Action Recommendations

Four actionable pillars addressing civil road engineering, speed calming, municipal enforcement, and institutional school policies.

🏗️

Pillar 1: Civil Engineering & Infrastructure

Rehabilitate pedestrian sidewalks, elevate crossing tables to kerb height, install tactile guidance for disabled students, and erect continuous protective guardrails.

🛑

Pillar 2: Traffic Calming & Speed Control

Introduce solar-powered blinking beacons, rubberized rumble strips 30m prior to school approaches, and convex blind-spot mirrors at congested T-junctions.

👮

Pillar 3: Enforcement & School Policy

Stagger kindergarten and secondary dispersal times, deploy trained school crossing marshals, designate pick-up bays, and ban commercial delivery trucks during bell hours.

🎓

Pillar 4: Education & Community Awareness

Conduct experiential pedestrian safety workshops for students, launch safe driver etiquette campaigns for parent carpools and local auto-rickshaw stands.

Direct Problem Mapping

Field Problems & Targeted Solutions

Every recommendation is directly derived from our on-ground hazard log and stakeholder survey feedback.

High Priority • Phase 1 (Immediate) Mapped Location: R01 (Main Gate)

Problem: Chaotic Double Parking & Absence of Drop-Off Bay

Private vehicles and auto-rickshaws double-park directly across the main entrance gates during 01:15–02:00 PM dismissal, forcing young children into active vehicular blind spots.

Engineered Remedy:
Demarcate a physical 50-meter passenger pick-up/drop-off bay 80 meters away from the primary school gate. Construct a raised tabletop pedestrian crossing with yellow thermoplastic markings and station two trained parent/school safety marshals with stop paddles during dismissal.
High Priority • Phase 1 (Immediate) Mapped Location: R02 (SV Road T-Junction)

Problem: High Vehicle Speeds on Blind Left Corner

Vehicles approach the corner at 42–48 km/h without slowing down. School approach lane has zero warning signs or pedestrian refuge islands.

Engineered Remedy:
Install a parabolic convex safety mirror on the corner wall, construct a series of 3 tactile rubber rumble strips 30 meters prior to the turning arc, and petition Mumbai Traffic Police for a push-button pelican pedestrian crossing signal.
Medium Priority • Phase 2 (Short-Term) Mapped Location: R03 (North Bus Stop)

Problem: Footpath Encroachment & Pavement Disrepair

Footpath width is squeezed to under 0.6 meters due to hawkers and broken paving slabs, forcing 78% of walking students onto the active vehicular carriageway.

Engineered Remedy:
Petition BMC Ward Office to enforce a 100-meter zero-vendor zone around the school gates, relay broken paver blocks with high-durability interlock tiles, and install continuous steel pedestrian guardrails separating sidewalk from moving traffic.
Medium Priority • Phase 2 (Short-Term) Mapped Location: R04 (Residential Intersection)

Problem: Foliage Obstruction & Missing School Zone Signage

Drivers entering from the residential road are unaware of school proximity due to overgrown trees blocking streetlights and total absence of regulatory warning signage.

Engineered Remedy:
Erect IRC standard retro-reflective fluorescent yellow-green 'Caution: School Ahead - Speed Limit 25 km/h' signs, prune overhanging tree foliage, and upgrade street lighting to 40W high-lumens LED luminaires.
Routine Upkeep • Regular Maintenance Mapped Location: R05 (South Approach)

Problem: Weathered Zebra Markings & Paint Erosion

Over 60% of zebra stripe paint has eroded from monsoon run-off. Motorists fail to yield because markings are invisible at 20 meters.

Engineered Remedy:
Repaint crosswalk stripes using heavy-duty thermoplastic reflective paint embedded with glass beads for night-time visibility, accompanied by yellow tactile kerb ramps for accessibility.
Institutional Policy Rule Mapped Location: R06 (Rear Service Gate)

Problem: Commercial & Waste Trucks Reversing During Dismissal

Delivery vans and municipal garbage trucks reverse into the narrow alleyway concurrently with junior kindergarten dismissal (12:15 PM) with zero reversing spotters.

Policy Remedy:
School administration must institute a strict commercial curfew: all canteen deliveries, heavy contractor vehicles, and municipal waste trucks are barred from entering or reversing between 07:00 AM–02:30 PM.
Phased Execution

Implementation Roadmap

Prioritizing recommendations by urgency, municipal approval timelines, and budget feasibility.

Phase 1: Months 0–3

Immediate Low-Cost Interventions

  • Erect 'School Zone 25 km/h' regulatory signs
  • Paint high-durability thermoplastic zebra stripes
  • Deploy trained school crossing wardens
  • Ban commercial delivery trucks during bell hours
Phase 2: Months 3–6

Civil Infrastructure Works

  • Rehabilitate broken footpath pavers
  • Construct tabletop raised pedestrian crossing
  • Install steel pedestrian guardrails
  • Clear vendor encroachments within 100m buffer
Phase 3: Long-Term

Smart City & Policy Reforms

  • Install push-button pelican traffic signals
  • Enforce speed camera zones on SV Road
  • Designate off-site park-and-walk corridors
  • Incorporate Safe Routes into Mumbai Master Plan
